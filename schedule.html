Class Descriptions

  • Beginner Boxing

    Learn the fundamentals of boxing in a supportive environment. No experience necessary.

  • Advanced Technique

    Refine your skills with advanced combinations and defensive techniques.

  • Conditioning Bootcamp

    High-intensity training to build strength, speed, and endurance.

  • Boxing Circuit

    Rotate through various boxing stations for a full-body workout.

  • Sparring Session

    Supervised sparring for intermediate and advanced boxers.

Class Policies

  • Reservations

    Classes can be booked up to 7 days in advance through our website or app.

  • Cancellations

    Please cancel at least 4 hours before class to avoid a late cancellation fee.

  • What to Bring

    Comfortable workout clothes, water bottle, and hand wraps. We provide gloves for beginners.

  • Arrival Time

    Please arrive 15 minutes before your first class for orientation.
